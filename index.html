A charming, 4-bedroom residence, offering privacy, space, and beautiful countryside views. Surrounded by mature, landscaped gardens and featuring well maintained outbuildings, this delightful home combines comfortable living with an idyllic rural lifestyle.

Set behind secure electric gates and approached by a sweeping tarmac driveway, this beautifully presented detached residence enjoys a private and idyllic setting on approximately 0.5acres, on mature grounds. The approach creates an immediate sense of tranquillity and exclusivity.

The property itself is a charming, two-storey home with a crisp façade and a thoughtfully designed layout. Large windows throughout flood the interiors with natural light while offering delightful views across the surrounding gardens.

The driveway, defined by landscaped gardens, offers ample parking and ease of access, while the gardens provide both open space for recreation and quieter, sheltered areas to relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Combining charm, privacy, and superb outdoor amenities, this is a rare opportunity to acquire a home that perfectly balances comfortable living with countryside lifestyle appeal.

The garden is a particularly impressive feature of the property, thoughtfully designed to make the most of its peaceful countryside setting. A generous workshop and sheds to the rear of the house, providing additional space for hobbies, car enthusiast or additional storage.

Location

Annacurra is the nearest village to the townland of Kilpipe, roughly 700meters from the property itself.

The village has a pub and a national (primary) school, known as St. Brigid's National School, Catholic Church, Public playground – with various modern equipment for kids including complex slides, swings, etc. and benches for parents. The Ciarán Shannon Walk is a looped trail that starts at the Church car park and follows purpose-built trails and forest roads that brings you back to the start at the car park. Annacurra GAA is an established Gaelic club with a strong history within County Wicklow and provides facilities and activities for both adult and underage players. Facilities in the area: Pilates, gym, yoga, indoor and outdoor bowls, plus other social activities.

It is a countryside area of Wicklow, on the western side of the Wicklow Mountains, with Croghan Mountain nearby to the east.

The property is conveniently located within easy reach of the M11 motorway, providing direct access north towards Dublin and the M50, and south towards Wexford and the southeast coast. Annacurra connects to the M11 via the R752 at Rathnew, making commuting and regional travel straightforward.
